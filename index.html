Our Story

Fine Indian cuisine with a modern twist

Frequently recommended as being the best Indian restaurant in Dorking, the Dorking Brasserie has been serving fine Indian cuisine with a modern twist since 2004.

We serve all your traditional favourites, but by far the most popular choices are the delicious Daily Specials. See the specials board for the choice of the day. If you'd like to be a little more adventurous, our staff will happily recommend something new to try. Owner Mr. Ali is famous for creating wonderful new dishes for you to savour.
